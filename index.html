FLUXBOUND

A physics toy that keeps a secret

You aim a stream. You open it. Things move — because they were pushed, not because a script said so.

Then the stream stops being water.

How it plays

One gesture

Hold anywhere. The stream aims where you point and pours while you hold. That is the whole control scheme, and it is enough.

Real momentum

Every particle carries mass and speed, and hands both to whatever it hits, at the point it hits. Off-centre means torque. Heavy means barely.

Materials answer back

Wood slides, metal ignores you, glass breaks, ice barely holds friction. Wet things get slippery. Hot things catch.

Nothing punishes you

Reset is instant. There is no score, no stars, no timer pressing on you. Weird solutions are the point.
